Time per GRPO step: alternating vs overlapped (projected from the measured slowdowns)

Alternating: rollout, then training, then the switch (offload, onload, adapter sync). Overlapped: training runs during the next rollout (one step stale), slowed by sharing the GPU; it lengthens the rollout by the generation share × (decode slowdown − 1) × the overlapped training time. Rollout minutes are the plan's estimates; training minutes come from the measured trainer speed for trained tokens per step.

Phases: inference only, training only, both together

Serving numbers exclude the first minute of each phase (warm-up). In the co-run phases, "with training" is calls whose first token falls inside the trainer's busy window; the rest serve as the same-server baseline. GPU activity is the DCGM mean over that window (fraction of time SMs / tensor cores / HBM are busy).
